Webhooks

When users or groups change, SCIMage sends a signed event to your systems so they can keep up. You set the destination with the SCIM_WEBHOOK_* settings when the server starts. Here you can watch delivery health and resend a parked event.

Parked and queued deliveries

Parked events used up their retries; SCIMage keeps each one so you can fix the receiver and replay it, which puts it back in line for a fresh round of attempts and records who did it in the admin audit log. Queued events are already back in line, whether from a recent replay or a normal retry, and stay listed here until they land or park again, instead of disappearing mid-flight.
