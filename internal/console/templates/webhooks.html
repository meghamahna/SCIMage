Webhooks

When users or groups change, SCIMage sends a signed event to your systems so they can keep up. You set the destination with the SCIM_WEBHOOK_* settings when the server starts. Here you can watch delivery health and resend a parked event.

Parked deliveries

Events that used up their retries. SCIMage keeps each one so you can fix the receiver and send it again. Replaying puts it back in line for a fresh round of attempts and records who did it in the admin audit log.
