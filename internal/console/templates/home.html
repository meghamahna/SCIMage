Where things live

  • Tenants — each customer you provision for, with their own users, groups, tokens, audit trail, and SCIM base URL.
  • Tokens — create and revoke the access tokens an identity provider signs in with.
  • Attributes — extra fields a tenant syncs on top of the standard SCIM set.
  • Webhooks — the health of the change events SCIMage sends to your systems, with the option to resend one.
  • Audit log — every provisioning change, per tenant. Admin audit covers what operators do.
  • ARIA — a helper that reviews the audit trail and points out patterns worth your attention. You stay in control of every decision.

This console is for the team that runs SCIMage. You can do everything here from the scimage-admin command line too. Console sign-in tokens are created from that command line.
